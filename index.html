About Us

Since 2024

Empowering Your Business with High-Value Services

At Integrated Compliance Solutions (PVT) LTD, we provide Management system certification, Consulting and Training services such as Auditor Training, Health and Safety training and consultancy services in Quality, Health & Safety, Environment, Food Safety and Information Security.

Courses are approved by professional bodies. Delegates are awarded internationally recognized certificates approved by organizations.

At Integrated Compliance Solutions, we deliver innovative, low-burden ISO compliance solutions and trainings that are customized and integrated to boost profitability, remove inefficiencies, and simplify system upkeep.

Since 2024, we have helped some of individuals; companies from start-ups to mature businesses develop digital and documented systems compliant to the most sought-after ISO standards and trainings in Pakistan.

Our TAX Consultancy Services Include

Tax Planning & Strategy: We work with businesses to develop effective tax strategies that align with their financial goals, reduce tax burdens, and take advantage of available incentives.

Compliance & Reporting: We assist with accurate tax filings, ensuring full compliance with all relevant tax authorities and regulations, both domestic and international.

Indirect Taxes: Guidance on VAT, sales tax, customs duties, and other indirect taxes, ensuring businesses meet their obligations and avoid penalties.

Transfer Pricing: We offer expertise in managing transfer pricing arrangements to meet global compliance standards and minimize the risk of audits.

Tax Dispute Resolution: In case of audits or disputes with tax authorities, we provide support and representation to help resolve issues efficiently and effectively.
